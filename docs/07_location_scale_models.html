9.1 Overview

This chapter presents the exploratory location-scale versions of the three main environmental model groups. These models keep the same mean component as the corresponding main environmental models, but also allow selected environmental predictors and methodological controls to enter the beta-binomial precision component.

In brms::beta_binomial(link_phi = "log"), positive coefficients in the precision component indicate higher precision and lower residual overdispersion, whereas negative coefficients indicate lower precision and higher residual overdispersion. These models are used as diagnostic and sensitivity checks rather than as the primary presentation set.

show_saved_summary <- function(summary_file, label) {
  if (!is.na(summary_file) && file.exists(summary_file)) {
    cat("<details><summary>", label, "</summary>\n\n```\n", sep = "")
    cat(readLines(summary_file), sep = "\n")
    cat("\n```\n</details>\n")
  } else {
    cat("Saved model summary is not available yet.")
  }
}

9.6 Location-scale model diagnostics

The location-scale models were fitted to evaluate whether environmental predictors and methodological controls also explained variation in the beta-binomial precision parameter. In brms::beta_binomial(link_phi = "log"), positive coefficients in the precision component indicate higher precision and lower residual overdispersion, whereas negative coefficients indicate lower precision and higher residual overdispersion.

The fitted location-scale models did not show sampler pathologies: no divergent transitions or maximum-treedepth hits were detected. However, the scale-component coefficients were generally estimated with wide credible intervals that overlapped zero. These results therefore provide limited support for interpreting predictor-specific changes in residual overdispersion, and the location-scale models are treated as exploratory rather than as the primary presentation set.

This chapter presents the exploratory location-scale versions of the three main environmental model groups. These models are used as diagnostic and sensitivity checks rather than as the primary presentation set.

## Location-scale model diagnostics

The location-scale models were fitted to evaluate whether environmental predictors and methodological controls also explained variation in the beta-binomial precision parameter. In `brms::beta_binomial(link_phi = "log")`, positive coefficients in the precision component indicate higher precision and lower residual overdispersion, whereas negative coefficients indicate lower precision and higher residual overdispersion.

The fitted location-scale models did not show sampler pathologies: no divergent transitions or maximum-treedepth hits were detected. However, the scale-component coefficients were generally estimated with wide credible intervals that overlapped zero. These results therefore provide limited support for interpreting predictor-specific changes in residual overdispersion, and the location-scale models are treated as exploratory rather than as the primary presentation set.
