7.1 Overview

This chapter defines the final primary environmental model set. The primary model set was organised around three environmental hypotheses.

  • The PCA models tested whether broad environmental-geographic gradients were associated with EPP.

  • The short-anomaly models tested whether recent deviations from local climatic conditions were associated with EPP.

  • The long-anomaly models tested whether deviations from the 1961-1989 climatic baseline were associated with EPP.

Each hypothesis group was fitted as a mean-only model and as a location-scale model, allowing environmental predictors to affect the expected EPP rate and, in the mean-scale version, allowing environmental predictors and methodological controls to affect residual dispersion. Methodological controls are nest_box and marker_type. All models use the same random-effect structure for population, non-phylogenetic species identity, and phylogenetic species covariance.

## Overview

This chapter defines the final primary environmental model set. The primary model set was organised around three environmental hypotheses.

- The PCA models tested whether broad environmental-geographic gradients were associated with EPP.

- The short-anomaly models tested whether recent deviations from local climatic conditions were associated with EPP.

- The long-anomaly models tested whether deviations from the 1961-1989 climatic baseline were associated with EPP.

Each hypothesis group was fitted as a mean-only model and as a location-scale model, allowing environmental predictors to affect the expected EPP rate and, in the mean-scale version, allowing environmental predictors and methodological controls to affect residual dispersion. Methodological controls are `nest_box` and `marker_type`. All models use the same random-effect structure for population, non-phylogenetic species identity, and phylogenetic species covariance.
