Main Aim

Test whether spatial and temporal variation in EPP is associated with climatic conditions, environmental variability, habitat productivity, and anomalies.

Inclusion Criteria
  • Socially monogamous species.
  • Known year or study period.
  • Known brood counts and EPP counts.
Model Note

Daily precipitation variability is log-transformed before standardisation.
