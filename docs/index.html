Author Aneta Arct, Santiago Ortega, Agnieszka Gudowska, Karolina Skorb, Monika Gronowska, Magdalena Zagalska-Neubauer, Dorota Kotowska, Szymon M. Drobniak, Malgorzata Lagisz, Shinichi Nakagawa
Published June 18, 2026

This book documents a reproducible comparative analysis of environmental variation and extra-pair paternity (EPP) in birds. The analysis uses population-level estimates of the proportion of sampled broods containing at least one extra-pair young and links them to environmental conditions, geography, species identity, phylogeny, and sampling structure.

The project is organised around three main environmental model groups: an environmental PCA model, a short-anomaly model, and a long-anomaly model. Each model group is fitted in two versions: a mean-only beta-binomial model and a location-scale beta-binomial model. All models include methodological controls for nest-box use and genetic marker type.

Main question

Do population-level EPP rates vary along broad environmental gradients related to temperature, precipitation, thermal variability, productivity, latitude, and distance to coast?
